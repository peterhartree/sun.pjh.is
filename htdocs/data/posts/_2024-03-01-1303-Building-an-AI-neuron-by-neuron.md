# Building an AI neuron by neuron

Imagine if, instead of using silicon, we built AI systems using biological matter. We scan someones brain and body in extreme detail, and then use nanobots to build an exact copy. The copy then starts behaving very much like the original person.

I think we'd assume that the copy is sentient, conscious, and just as much as moral patient as the original. We'd also hesitate to make billions of copies, until we were confident they'd enjoy their lives and not cause undue trouble.

The "biological copy" thought experiment pumps more confident intuitions than a "silicon copy" version. Its on us to say, where and why we should not bring those intuitions to the "silicon copy" version.
