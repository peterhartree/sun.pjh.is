# Ronald Dworkin on Posner's doubts about the persuasiveness of moral arguments

Posner declares, as a matter of _a priori_ psychological dogma, that moral arguments, no matter how sound or powerful, never convince anyone not already convinced anyway [...] no doubt many people are never moved by the logic of a moral argument, even once in their lives, but it is absurd to suppose that no one ever is.
