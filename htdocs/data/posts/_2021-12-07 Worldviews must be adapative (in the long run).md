# Worldviews must be adapative (in the long run)

As Robin Hanson [recently](https://www.overcomingbias.com/2021/12/on-evolved-values.html) put it:

> Evolution selects roughly for creatures that do whatever it takes to have more descendants in the long run.

This imposes a longterm constraint on our thinking about value, about what we owe to each other, and so on. 

If a cluster of views significantly reduces the reproduction rates of those who hold them, it will gradually disappear. 

Usually we consider an action "right" if it is in line with widely accepted norms. But at the second order—the matter of determining the widely held norms of a group—adaptiveness constrains the long-run possibilities.
