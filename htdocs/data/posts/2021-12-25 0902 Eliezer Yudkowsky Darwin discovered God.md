# Eliezer Yudkowsky: Darwin discovered God

In a way, Darwin _discovered_ God—a God that failed to match the preconceptions of theology, and so passed unheralded. If Darwin had discovered that life was created by an intelligent agent—a bodiless mind that loves us, and will smite us with lightning if we dare say otherwise—people would have said "My gosh! That's God!"

But instead Darwin discovered a strange alien God—not comfortably "ineffable", but _really genuinely different from us_. Evolution is not a God, but if it were, it wouldn't be Jehovah. It would be H. P. Lovecraft's [Azathoth](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Azathoth), the blind idiot God burbling chaotically at the center of everything, surrounded by the thin monotonous piping of flutes.
