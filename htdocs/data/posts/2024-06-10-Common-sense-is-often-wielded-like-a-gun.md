# ”Common sense is often wielded like a gun”
That’s [Stephen West on Žižek](https://www.philosophizethis.org/transcript/episode-200-transcript)—on the most popular kind of overconfidence. 

The philosopher seeks “cracks in the symbolic edifice that grounds our social stability.” And yeah, most people resist such thoughts (correctly).
