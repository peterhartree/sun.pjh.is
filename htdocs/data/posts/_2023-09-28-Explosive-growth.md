# 
Let's say we expect to enter a period of explosive economic growth sometime in the next 3-30 years, due to the ability of AI systems to perform most human labor. That'd be a period where the economy grows at 30% a year instead of 3% a year, such that it doubles in size every ~2 years, rather than every ~20.

What should an individual do to prepare for that? Some ideas:

1. Buy global market ETFs: their value should roughly track the growth of the global economy.
2. Buy some sector-specific ETFs, which seem likely to outperform global market ETFs. What kinds of industries seem likely to become very valuable in those worlds? Semiconductors, energy, data-centers, robotics.
3. Buy volatility stocks: e.g. defence companies.
