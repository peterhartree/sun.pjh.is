# Intuition and explicit reasoning are limited tools, so be more uncertain
An acquaintaince once said something like:

> The way conservatives tend to trust their moral intuitions seems arrogant to me. After all, they're just _your_ intuitions. Surely you should try to reflect on them explicitly?

The conservative would reply: where do your intuitions come from? Your intuitions are shaped by the culture you grew up in, and that culture has been shaped over millenia.
