How to personally prepare for the coming wave. Some ideas: 
1. Buy index funds, plus some volatility stocks.
2. Learn about AI. Understand most likely scenarios for 1-10 years. What skills and resources will be scarce and valuable?
3. Start or join projects that seem likely to do well over this period.
4. Improve your skills.

Disaster prep:
1. Spend a day or two preparing to be self-reliant for a couple of weeks, while society recovers from a major shock. Think: a worse pandemic, a cyber disaster.
2. Ideally, have an isolated place in the countryside?
