# Jason Matheny on moving fast and not breaking things

Because technology moves so much faster than policy, I think we’re going to need to make much greater investments in things like technology forecasts, stronger forms of risk assessment, a rejection of, you know, the sort of Silicon Valley ethos of moving fast and breaking things. We can’t afford to move fast and break things on synthetic biology or AI. We need a much greater emphasis on public safety because the consequences of screwing up could be catastrophic.
